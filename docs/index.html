What else it handles

  • Progress you can watch

    Percent, speed and time left update while the file downloads, on Windows as well.

  • Picks up copied links

    Copy a link in your browser, switch to yoinks, and it offers to use that link.

  • Audio as MP3

    Choose Audio only to keep just the sound. ffmpeg converts it on your computer.

  • Stays on your computer

    There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Files go to your Downloads folder, or any folder you pick.

  • Keeps up with site changes

    yoinks updates yt-dlp, the engine inside it, so downloads keep working after YouTube or TikTok change something.

  • Light and dark

    The app follows your system theme, and you can switch it yourself at the bottom of the window.

Questions people ask

Is yoinks really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license. There are no ads, no account and no paid version.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe to continue?

Windows shows that warning because the app is not code-signed yet, and signing certificates cost money every year. Click More info, then Run anyway. Every line of code is public on GitHub, and GitHub Actions builds the downloads from that code.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Not as a download yet. The macOS build targets Apple Silicon (M1 and newer). If you have an Intel Mac, you can build yoinks from the source code with Node.js by following the README.

YouTube says "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot". What now?

YouTube sometimes blocks downloads from internet connections it thinks are automated. The block comes from YouTube and usually clears after a while. Other sites are not affected.

Is downloading videos legal?

It depends on the video and on where you live. Download your own uploads, public domain and Creative Commons films, or anything the owner lets you keep. yoinks does not break DRM, so paid streaming services will not work.

Where do my downloads go?

To your Downloads folder. Click Change under the link box in the app to pick another folder.
